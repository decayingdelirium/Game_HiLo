##Welcome to a C++ CLI recreation of HiLo

In this game, you will guess a number between 0 and 100, and then a machine will evaluate whether your guess is higher or lower than the randomly generated number!

##Instructions on how to play without downloading:
Visit https://onlinegdb.com/Km13rdQlJ
Click the giant green Run button at the top of the webpage to play
